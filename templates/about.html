What BAH is

BAH is a housing allowance for a member assigned in the 50 states or D.C. when government quarters are not provided. It depends on the duty location, pay grade, and whether the member has dependents. DoD says it is not intended to cover all housing costs. The member can spend more or less than the rate. DTMO's BAH page is the program description. Entitlement rules are in FMR Volume 7A, Chapter 26.

Individual rate protection, in DTMO's words, keeps a member from receiving a lower rate than the year before while eligibility stays the same. It stops when status changes, including a PCS, a reduction in grade, or a change in dependency. This site does not apply rate protection to your pay. It shows the published table.

BAH-Differential, partial BAH, and reserve-component or transient rates are not in this locality file. Ask finance, or read the FMR, for those.
