    How a lookup works

    1. DoD maps the duty ZIP to a military housing area (an MHA code) or to a county cost group.
    2. That code has one monthly rate per pay grade with dependents, and one without.
    3. The PCS checklist is a list of costs to price. It does not tell you what to spend.
